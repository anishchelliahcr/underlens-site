Underlens Privacy Policy

Effective November 1, 2026

Underlens has no accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking, and the app sends nothing to the developer.

Your scripts and settings

Scripts and settings are stored only on your Mac, inside the app's private folder. Delete a script in the app to remove it. Deleting the app and its data removes everything.

Microphone

The microphone is used only while the prompter is playing in Voice or Words mode, or when you press Test Microphone. Voice mode measures loudness only. Audio is never saved by the app.

Speech recognition

Words mode uses Apple's speech recognition to compare what you say with your script. Underlens runs it on your Mac when your language supports that. If it doesn't, your speech is sent to Apple's speech service to be transcribed, under Apple's privacy policy. Settings › Scrolling shows which applies to your language. Recognised text is used only to follow your script and is not saved.

Your choices

You can turn off microphone or speech recognition access at any time in System Settings › Privacy & Security. Steady scrolling needs neither.

Third parties

Underlens contains no third-party SDKs. The only outside service it can use is Apple's speech service, as described above.
