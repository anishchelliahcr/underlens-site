Words mode doesn't follow me

Check that Siri or Dictation is turned on in System Settings, and that Underlens is allowed under Privacy & Security › Microphone and Speech Recognition. In Settings › Scrolling, make sure the language matches your script.

A keyboard shortcut does nothing

Another app may already use it. Settings › Shortcuts marks any shortcut that's in use elsewhere.

Can people see the prompter when I share my screen?

Underlens asks macOS to leave the prompter out of screen capture, but capture apps can behave differently. Try a test share with the app you present in before relying on it.
